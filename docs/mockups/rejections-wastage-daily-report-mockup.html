    Days with no rejection, wastage or leakage entries are skipped, so Sundays and holidays never raise alerts.

    Lifecycle

    Draft→ Submitted→ Approved Rejected by super admin goes back to the manager, then re-submitted.
    Approved reports are locked. Only a super admin can reopen one, and the reopen is logged.

    Deadlines Pakistan time · editable by super admin

    DAY END + 24 h

    Reminder to the rejections manager (bell + push) if the report is still a draft.

    DAY END + 48 h

    Submit overdue. Super admins are alerted and the day shows red on the dashboard.

    SUBMITTED + 48 h

    Approval overdue. Super admins are alerted once a day at 09:00 until approved or sent back.

    REJECTED + 48 h

    Re-submit overdue. Same alert path as a late submission.

    Day end is midnight after the report date. The check runs every 15 minutes, the same way as the key raw material remarks.
