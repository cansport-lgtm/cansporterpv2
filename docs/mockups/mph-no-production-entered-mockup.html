Submit rules

Flag openReasons locked for that department. Save Draft works. Submit is blocked, on the form and in the database.
Entry fixedFigures refresh, the flag clears, and the department is treated as an ordinary loss or no loss.
Confirmed no productionOnly reasons in the new "No production" category can be picked. Remarks are required. Who confirmed and when is saved and printed.
