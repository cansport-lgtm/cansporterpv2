How the module connects to the rest of the ERP

Fixed Assets (office_assets) ──► Asset Custody (holder, condition, verification) ──► HR clearance on exit └─► Cameras / Equipment register (optional fixed_asset_id) Items master (office_supplies) ──► Admin store stock ◄── GRN (office supplies PO) Employee ──► Supply Request SR ──► dept head (above free qty) ──► issue from store Admin store below reorder ──► Purchase Request PRQ (existing flow) ──► PO ──► GRN Camera daily check ──(line not ok)──► Equipment ticket EQT ──► cost ──► Petty cash / General expense Cleaning inspection ──(not ok)──► Action item (cleaning supervisor) · equipment faults ──► EQT Equipment intervals / AMC ──► Service calendar ──► preventive EQT ──► alerts (System Notifications) SIM register ──► monthly bill ──► Expenses › Utility bills (over-limit borne by company, flagged to manager)

Delivery

StepContainsTouches
1Module + roles, dashboard, camera daily check, cleaning inspections and action items, masters, alertsNothing else
2Asset custody, admin store, supply requests, reorder → purchase requestFixed Assets (read), Purchase (creates PRQ)
3SIM register and bills, over-limit report, HR clearance bannerExpenses, HR
4Equipment register, tickets, service calendar, AMC alerts, camera fault → ticketExpenses (petty cash)
